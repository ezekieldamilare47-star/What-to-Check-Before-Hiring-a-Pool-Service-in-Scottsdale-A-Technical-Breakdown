## Warning Signs a Pool Needs Attention

- Signs that call for repair rather than routine cleaning include a pump that is noisy or loses prime, a filter that no longer keeps the water clear, a heater that does not reach temperature, cracked tile or plaster, and lights that flicker or fail.
- Unexplained water loss or wet spots near equipment

Comparing quotes works best when the scope matches.
